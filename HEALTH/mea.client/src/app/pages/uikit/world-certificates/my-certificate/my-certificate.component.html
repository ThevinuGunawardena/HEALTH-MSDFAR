The official inspector certifies that:

a) Fishery and aquaculture products have been caught and handled in accordance with health and hygiene requirements laid down in the Fish Products (Export) Regulation 1998 of Sri Lanka;

b) Fishery and/or aquaculture products were landed, handled, packaged, prepared, processed, frozen, stored and transported in a hygienic – sanitary way in establishments officially approved by the competent health authority, in compliance with the organoleptic, parasitological, chemical, virological or microbiological criteria of the exporting country and under conditions at least equivalent to the Standards and Codes of Practice established in the Codex Alimentarius ;

d) This consignment of fish and fishery products meets all the quality and safety requirements stipulated by the Fish Products (Export) Regulation 1998 of Sri Lanka and are in compliance with organoleptic and microbiological criteria of the said regulations and where aquacultured products are concerned such products are subjected to a national aquaculture chemical residue monitoring programme in accordance with the Aquaculture (Monitoring of Residues) Regulation 2002 of Sri Lanka and therefore, fit for human consumption;

e) The material used for packaging meets the food safety requirements stipulated in the national fish products export regulations;

f) The cargo to be exported has a printed identification which certifies that these products come from the establishment stated in this certificate and is labeled correctly.
